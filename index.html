How to install

KustomoImprenta is downloaded straight from this page, not from the Play Store.

  1. Download on your Android phoneOpen this page on your phone and tap Download for Android. On a computer? Scan the QR code with your phone's camera.
  2. Open the downloaded fileTap the notification, or open Files → Downloads and tap KustomoImprenta.apk.
  3. Allow the installIf your phone asks, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source for your browser, then go back.
  4. Tap InstallIf Google Play Protect warns that the app isn't from the Play Store, tap More details → Install anyway.
  5. Open KustomoImprenta and sign inCreate an account or sign in with Google, and start designing.

Questions

Is KustomoImprenta free?

Yes. The app is free; you only pay for the shirts you order.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. KustomoImprenta is only available for Android phones for now.

Why does my phone warn me before installing?

Android shows that warning for every app that isn't installed from the Play Store. Download KustomoImprenta only from this page.

How do I update the app?

Download it again from this page and install it over the old one. Your account and orders stay the same.
