    Export a copy first
    Deleting here can't be undone. Download a copy on the Export page first. Tick what was made for testing, then press Delete selected at the bottom. Admin accounts, the built-in shirts and the keys on App Keys are never touched here.
    Two things this page can't remove: sign-in accounts (delete them in Firebase → Authentication) and uploaded images (delete the kustomoimprenta/… folders in the Cloudinary console).
    Fresh start for the shop

    Handing the system over? This ticks everything below: every order, customer profile, product, design preset, category and added model, the staff's own test designs, the usage counts, and Shop Settings (back to the defaults). Nothing is deleted until you press Delete selected and type DELETE.
