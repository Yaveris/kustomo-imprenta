    One-time Firestore setup

    Firestore isn't letting this page read or save models yet. Until it does, the app shows only the five built-in shirts. Open the Firestore rules, paste this inside match /databases/{database}/documents { … }, press Publish, then reload this page. 

        // Models & Try-On page: app users read, shop staff change
    match /models/{id} {
      allow read: if request.auth != null;
      allow write: if request.auth != null && get(
        /databases/$(database)/documents/users/$(request.auth.uid)
      ).data.role in ['admin', 'super_admin'];
    }

    For your 3D artist and developer

    Send this to your 3D artist

    "Please make a 3D model of our [item] that we can upload to our shop's app. It needs to be:"

    • a .glb file (glTF Binary), under 8 MB and under 60,000 triangles, textures up to 2048 px;
    • real size in metres, Y up, front facing +Z;
    • with its origin where it touches the body (the middle of a cap's band, the bridge of glasses, the middle of a strap);
    • with the printable surface as its own part named PrintFront (and PrintBack if the back can be printed), about 1 mm off the surface and UV-mapped to fill the whole 0–1 square;
    • in white or light grey if customers can pick its colour.

    A sample set up this way is in the project at lens-studio/models/SampleCap.glb.

    What works straight away, and what needs your developer

    • Things that don't bend (caps, hats, glasses, necklaces, bags, watches) work as soon as they're uploaded: in the app's 3D view, the designer and AR try-on.
    • AR try-on for added models needs the try-on Lens published once with model downloads, then Try-on for added models turned on above. Until then the app hides AR try-on for them.
    • Clothes that bend with the body, like the five shirts, also work in the 3D view and designer once uploaded. For the try-on to wrap around the wearer, your developer adds them in Lens Studio:
    Steps for your developer (Lens Studio)
    1. Open lens-studio/your-project/your-project.esproj in Lens Studio.
    2. Import the garment as an External Mesh on a Body Mesh, set up like the objects under Shirts: one object with Shirt, Print Front and Print Back children.
    3. Name it with no spaces (for example DenimJacket) and put it under Shirts.
    4. Publish the Lens and add it to the Camera Kit Lens Group (see App Keys).
    5. Here, open the model and type that name in Built into the Lens as.
