    2 · Adding a product

    Everything customers can buy is a product. Each product is one shirt style in one design idea, with its price, sizes and (if you count it) the stock in storage.

    10 minutes

    Step by step

    1. Open Products & Stock and press Add Product. If a shirt style has nothing for sale yet, a button for it at the top of the form fills it in for you.
    2. Type the name customers will see, pick a category (the shelf it sits on) and the price.
    3. Choose whether customers can change the design: Customers can change it or Ready-made (lesson 4).
    4. Add a photo, or click a ready-made design under the photo box to use a picture of it. No photo? The app shows the 3D shirt instead.
    5. Pick the sizes and decide whether to count the stock (lesson 5).
    6. Press Save. A new product goes straight to the Design Studio to make its design.
    7. Check that For sale in the app is on. It's in the app at once.

    What each part of the form does

    Name and descriptionWhat customers read on the product page. Keep the name short, like “Team Ballers Jersey Tee”.
    CategoryThe shelf in the app (Tees, Hoodies…). Set the shelves up with Categories.
    Price and discountThe regular price, and an optional % off. The app shows the old price crossed out with a “-20%” badge.
    For sale in the appOff hides it from customers without deleting it. Good for seasonal items.
    DesignWhether customers can change your design, or buy it exactly as made.
    PhotoThe picture in the shop list. A picture of the design works well.
    Sizes and stockThe sizes offered, and either “made to order” or how many of each are in storage.
    Advanced: 3D modelWhich shirt shape the app uses for the 3D view, the designer and AR try-on. Usually set by the name.
    Advanced: tag and measurementsA New / Sale / Hot badge, and the size chart customers see when choosing a size.
    Practise safely: the Add example products button on Products & Stock adds 10 sample products, two for each shirt style, marked “Example”. Edit them as much as you like, then press Remove the examples before the shop opens.

    3 · Pricing, discounts, shipping and downpayments

    What a customer pays is the product price (after any discount) times the quantity, plus shipping. Big orders can pay part now and the rest on delivery.

    8 minutes

    The rules

    • Lowest price ₱20. GCash doesn't accept smaller payments, so the form won't save a price or discount below that.
    • Discounts are a whole % from 0 to 90. The price is rounded down to whole pesos, so customers never save less than shown.
    • Shipping is one fee per order, and free from a set subtotal. Both are set in Shop Settings (Super Admin).
    • Downpayment: carts with enough items can pay a part now through GCash. The rest is collected on delivery and marked on the order (lesson 8).

    4 · Designs: starting designs and ready-made products

    The shop makes designs in the Design Studio. A product's design is used in one of two ways, chosen on the product form.

    12 minutes

    Starting design · customers can change it

    The product opens in the app's designer with your design already on it. Customers change the words, move things, add their own pictures and pick any shirt colour. Best for group shirts, birthdays, school batches and business uniforms.

    Ready-made · can't be changed

    Sold exactly as you designed it, in its own colour. Customers only pick the size and quantity; the app shows “Ready-made design, printed exactly as shown”. Best for your own collections, seasonal prints and stock you've already printed.

    Making a product's design

    1. On the product's card, press Create one (or Make the design for a ready-made product). The Design Studio opens with that product's shirt.
    2. Add text, a picture or shapes. Drag them into place inside the dashed print area. Use Front / Back to switch sides.
    3. Pick the shirt colour. For a ready-made product, this is the colour it's sold in.
    4. Press Save. The product card shows a small picture of it, and customers see it at once.
    Designs you'll reuse belong in the Design Library: customers pick them under Shop designs in the app's designer. The library has 12 starter designs you can add with one click. Fonts and limits for the designer are on Studio Settings.

    5 · Stock

    Each product is either made to order (any size, any quantity) or counted: you enter how many of each size are in storage and the system keeps the count.

    6 minutes
    • Counting is automatic. When an order comes in, its pieces are taken off the moment any admin page is open. Each order is counted once only.
    • Cancelling gives stock back. Setting an order to Cancelled returns its pieces to storage.
    • Customers can't over-buy. The app shows how many are left and greys out sold-out sizes.
    • Low and out of stock sizes turn yellow and red. The level counted as “low” is set in Shop Settings.

    Daily stock work

    1. Press In storage at the top of Products & Stock to see every count, with the low ones first.
    2. New delivery from your supplier? Press Stock on the product and use +, or type the count after counting the shelf.
    3. Print shirts only when ordered? Turn off Count how many are in storage, and it's made to order.

    6 · Orders

    Paid orders arrive in Orders with a badge in the menu. Open one to see the customer, the items with their designs and print files, the delivery address, the chat and its history.

    12 minutes

    The statuses

    StatusMeansWhat you do
    ⏳ PendingPaid and waiting for you.Check the items, sizes and designs. Ask in the chat if anything is unclear.
    ⚙️ ProcessingAccepted, being prepared.Download the print files and get the shirts ready.
    🖨️ PrintingOn the press.Print. From here, the order can't be cancelled by the customer.
    📦 ReadyPacked, waiting for pickup or delivery.Assign a driver (lesson 8).
    ✅ DeliveredIn the customer's hands.Collect any balance first and mark it.
    ✖️ CancelledNot going ahead.Refund through PayMongo if paid. Stock goes back automatically.

    Inside an order

    • Order items: each item with its size, colour and design. A ready-made product shows its ready-made design.
    • Print files: each side as a PNG at its real printed size. Use these for printing, not screenshots.
    • Fit Scan measurements, when the customer sent them, show with the item.
    • Staff notes: notes only staff see, like “use the heavier cotton”.
    • Status history: who changed what and when.

    7 · Chats with customers

    Every order has its own chat. Customers write from My Orders in the app, and you answer from the order page or from Messages, which lists every chat with the unread ones first.

    5 minutes
    • A number on Messages in the menu shows unread chats.
    • You and the customer can send pictures, such as a design proof or a photo of a misprint.
    • Use the chat to confirm details before printing, and to tell the customer about delays or the driver.
    • Cancellations and reprints are agreed in the chat too (see the shop's Terms in Shop Settings).
    Good habit: answer every chat on the same day, and send a proof picture for big group orders before moving them to Printing.
    Open Messages

    8 · Delivery

    Customers pin their address on a map when they order. The order page shows where they are, how far from the shop, and the driver you send.

    8 minutes
    1. Set the shop's location once (Getting Started, step 2). Every order then shows the distance from your door.
    2. When the order is Ready, open its Delivery box: the address, the map, the distance by road and a Directions button for Google Maps.
    3. Assign a driver: their name, phone and plate or booking number (a Lalamove booking number works too). Drivers you've used before are suggested.
    4. Press Tell the customer. It writes a message with the driver's details in the order chat; check it and press send.
    5. Paid with a downpayment? The order shows the Balance on delivery. Once the driver collects it, press Mark as collected.
    6. When it arrives, set the status to Delivered.
    A wrong pin means a lost driver. If the map pin looks far from the typed address, ask the customer in the chat before sending anyone.
