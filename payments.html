    Payments GCash

    Customers pay with GCash through your PayMongo account. Here you connect that account, see every payment, and give refunds. The money goes to the PayMongo account whose key is saved below.

    PayMongo account
    Checking…

    From dashboard.paymongo.com → Developers → API Keys, copy the Secret key (it starts with sk_live_, or sk_test_ for trying things out). Paste it here and press Save. For safety it can't be shown again after saving, only replaced.

    Setup (done once by whoever installs the system)

    The payment server keeps the key off customers' phones and saves each order the moment PayMongo confirms it. Paste its address here once (from Render, like https://kustomo-payments.onrender.com).

    Steps for setting up the server on Render are in the FitFusionServer/README.md file.
