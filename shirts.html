    Shirts & Fabrics

    The shirts your shop prints on. Fabrics explain each material to customers (how it feels, what it suits, how to care for it). Brands are the shirts you stock, each made of a fabric and with its own price per piece. Each product chooses which brands customers can pick (edit a product → Shirts), and the brand's price is added to the product's printing price at checkout.

    View only
    Only the shop owner (Super Admin) adds or changes fabrics, brands and prices.
