    Help How to run the shop

    Click a task to see the steps. Setting up the shop for the first time? Start with Getting Started.

    Every day
    Handle a new order
    1. Open Orders. New orders are under Pending, and the number beside Orders in the menu shows how many are waiting.
    2. Click the order to see the items, sizes, the customer's address and the design they sent.
    3. Check each item's picture and design. If the customer asks to cancel or change the design (they can until Printing), answer with the buttons on their request in the chat.
    4. Change the status with the box at the top as you work: Processing → Printing → Ready for pickup → Delivered. The customer sees each change in the app.
    5. Press Print for a job sheet to keep with the order.

    Stock comes off by itself as soon as an order comes in, and goes back if you cancel it.

    Send an order out with a driver
    1. Open the order. The Delivery box shows the address, the phone number, how far it is from your shop and a map.
    2. Press Copy address to paste it into a courier app or a message, or Directions to open the route in Google Maps.
    3. Type the driver's name and phone (and the plate or booking number if you have it), then press Assign driver.
    4. Press Tell the customer to put the driver's details in the chat, check it, and send.

    The Orders list shows who has a driver. The No driver yet tab lists the orders still waiting for one.

    Collect the balance of a downpayment order
    1. Downpayment orders show Balance due in the Orders list.
    2. When the customer pays the rest on delivery or pickup, open the order and press Mark as collected next to Balance.
    3. Setting an order to Delivered also asks whether the balance was collected.

    "Money received" on the Dashboard counts GCash payments plus the balances you mark as collected.

    Reply to a customer
    1. Open Messages. Conversations with something new have an orange dot.
    2. Click a conversation, type your reply and press Enter. The grey buttons above the box are ready-made replies you can use as-is or edit.
    3. Each order has its own conversation, so you can also reply from inside the order.
    Cancel an order and refund it
    1. Open the order and set the status to Cancelled. Its stock is put back.
    2. If the customer paid, refund them from the PayMongo dashboard (Payments → the payment → Refund).
    3. Let the customer know in the order chat.
    Catalog
    Add or change a product
    1. Open Products & Stock and press Add Product, or Edit on a product. Duplicate makes a copy to change, like a new colour.
    2. Fill in the name, category, price (at least ₱20) and sizes.
    3. To put it on sale, type a Discount (%). The Customers pay box shows the new price as you type.
    4. For the photo, upload one or click a ready-made design made on the same shirt.
    5. Turn on Count how many are in storage and set each size with − and +.
    6. Rarely needed, under Advanced: the 3D model, a tag on the photo, and the measurements per size customers see in the app.
    7. Press Save. Use the For sale switch to hide a product from the app without deleting it.
    Organise products into categories
    1. Open Products & Stock and press Categories at the top.
    2. Type a name and press Add. Use the arrows to set the order they appear in the app.
    3. Pick each product's category when you add or edit it.
    Add stock when a delivery arrives, or check what's in storage
    1. Open Products & Stock and choose In storage.
    2. For a delivery, press Add a delivery on the product, type how many of each size came in, and press Add to storage.
    3. For one or two pieces, use − and +. After counting the shelf, click a number and type what you counted.
    4. The boxes at the top show what's running low or out of stock; click one to see only those. Count sheet downloads a list to check the shelves with.

    Orders take stock off by themselves as they come in; cancelling an order puts it back. When a size is out of stock the app stops selling it.

    Make a shop design customers can add
    1. Open Design Studio and choose Shop designs.
    2. Add text (any of the shirt fonts, curves, outlines, effects), shapes, a text layout or an image, on any side.
    3. Fill in its name, category and search words, choose whether customers may change its words or colours, and press Publish shop design. Customers find it under Designs › Shop designs.
    4. To set the design a product opens with, choose Product starting design, pick the product and press Save to product.

    Have a folder of artwork? Design Library → Upload & bulk import turns each picture (or a .zip of them) into a design. The library also publishes, hides, reorders and schedules designs, and Usage shows which ones customers order.

    Choose the fonts, layouts, tools and inks customers get
    1. Open Studio Settings. The Standard set is what every product uses.
    2. Change its fonts (tick, or star to recommend; upload your own), text layouts, drawing and photo tools, ink colours and print check limits, then Save settings.
    3. For different rules on some products (for example only your vinyl colours on caps), press New set and choose the products or the category it is for. Each product uses exactly one set; the page lists which.
    Get the print files for an order
    1. Open the order. Each side of each design shows a preview, the inks it needs, and anything the app's print check warned the customer about.
    2. Press PNG or PDF on a side, or All print files (ZIP) for the item. Files are made at the side's real size (set the resolution, and Mirror for transfers, above the items).

    For a model you added on the 3D Models page, enter the real width of its print area (cm) so its print files come out the right size.

    Super Admin
    Add or remove an admin
    1. Open Admin Accounts and press Create Admin.
    2. Enter their name, email and a starting password, and give them the password in person.
    3. To stop someone's access, turn off their switch. To give a customer's account admin access, create an admin with their email.
    Change the shipping fee, downpayment or Terms
    1. Open Shop Settings.
    2. Change the amounts. The box "What customers see" shows the result.
    3. Press Save settings. The app uses them within a few seconds; orders already placed keep their amounts.
    Set the shop's location
    1. Open Shop Settings and press Set the location under Shop location.
    2. Search your address or press Use where I am, drag the pin onto your door, and press Save location.

    Every order then shows the distance from the shop. An admin who isn't a Super Admin can set one from an order page; it's saved for their account only.

    Keep a backup of the shop's data
    1. Open the Advanced group in the menu, then Export.
    2. Press Export data and keep the file somewhere safe. Do this before any big change.
    3. For a spreadsheet of orders, use Export on the Orders page.
    Forgot your password
    1. On the sign-in page, type your email and press Forgot password?.
    2. Open the link in the email (check spam) and choose a new password.

    The admin site signs you out after 30 minutes without activity, to protect a shared computer.
