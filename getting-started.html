    Part 1 · Set up the shop

    about 30 minutes, here on this site

    Everything in this part is done from this admin site. No technical knowledge needed.

    1

    Check the shipping fee and payment rules Super Admin

    What customers pay for delivery, when shipping is free, and whether big orders can pay half now.

    1. Open Shop Settings.
    2. Change the amounts. The box What customers see shows the result as you type.
    3. Under Terms & Privacy Policy, press Start from the built-in text, read it, and change anything that doesn't fit your shop.
    4. Press Save settings.
    Open Shop Settings
    2

    Set your shop's location

    Each order then shows how far the customer is from you, with directions for the driver.

    1. Press Set the location below.
    2. Type your shop's address and pick it from the list, or press Use where I am if you're at the shop.
    3. Drag the pin onto your door, then press Save location.
    3

    Add your products and how many you have

    What customers can buy in the app, with prices, sizes and the stock in storage.

    1. Open Products & Stock and press Categories to set up your shelves (for example Tees, Hoodies, Caps).
    2. Press Add Product. Fill in the name, category, price and sizes.
    3. Turn on Count how many are in storage and type how many of each size you have. Each order takes them off by itself.
    4. Press Save. Repeat for each product.
    Open Products & Stock
    4

    Make a few ready-made designs

    Customers who don't have a design can start from one of yours.

    1. Open Design Studio and choose Presets.
    2. Add text, a shape or a picture on the shirt.
    3. Press Publish preset. It appears in the app's designer straight away.
    Open Design Studio
    5

    Add your staff Super Admin

    Give each person their own sign-in, so you can switch one off without changing everyone's password.

    1. Open Admin Accounts and press Create Admin.
    2. Type their name, email and a starting password. Tell them the password in person, not by message.
    Open Admin Accounts
    6

    Share the app with your customers

    Customers install the app from your download page. It has a QR code you can print and stick on the counter.

    1. Open the download page and check that the button downloads the app.
    2. Share its link on your Facebook page, or print the QR code.
    Open the download page

    Part 2 · The accounts behind the app

    one time only

    The app uses a few outside services. Each one should be in the shop's own name, so the shop owns its customers, pictures and payments. Where it says your developer, they can do it with you in a short call.

    Firebase: your database and sign-ins

    Firebase keeps the shop's records: customer accounts, products, orders and messages. Think of it as the shop's filing cabinet.

    Free for a shop this sizeYou and your developer10 minutes
    1. Use a Google account that belongs to the shop (make one at accounts.google.com if needed).
    2. Your developer adds that account as the project's Owner. You get an email from Firebase: open it and press Accept.
    3. Sign in at console.firebase.google.com and check that you can see the project.
    4. Your developer then removes their own access. Nothing in the app or this site changes.
    Turn on 2-Step Verification for that Google account. Whoever controls it controls the shop's data.
    For your developer

    Project settings → Users and permissions → Add member (role Owner). The database rules are on App Keys → One-time Firestore setup; publish them once after any update to this site.

    Picture storage (Cloudinary) Super Admin

    Stores the pictures customers put on their designs, and your product photos.

    Free plan is enough to startYou10 minutes
    1. Sign up at cloudinary.com with the shop's email.
    2. On the Cloudinary home screen, copy your Cloud name.
    3. Click the gear (Settings) → Upload → Add upload preset. Set Signing mode to Unsigned, press Save, and copy the preset's name.
    4. Open App Keys, paste both, press Test upload, then Save Cloudinary keys.
    Only the cloud name and the preset name go in App Keys. Never paste Cloudinary's "API secret" anywhere.
    Open App Keys

    Maps: nothing to set up Ready

    The app's delivery map and address search, and this site's distances, use OpenStreetMap. It's free and needs no account, key or credit card.

    FreeNothing to sign up for
    1. Just set your shop's location (Part 1, step 2).

    AR Try-On (Snapchat Camera Kit) Super Admin

    Lets customers point their phone camera at themselves and see the shirt on them. It runs on Snapchat's Camera Kit, so it needs a Snapchat business account.

    FreeYou, with your developer for step 430 minutes
    1. Sign in at my-lenses.snapchat.com with the shop's Snapchat account and create an Organization for the shop.
    2. Turn on Camera Kit and create an app. If it asks for the package name, type com.ksprintexpress.fitfusion.
    3. Copy the app's Production API token.
    4. Your developer opens the try-on project in Lens Studio (Snapchat's free program for computers) and publishes it to your Organization. This is done once.
    5. In My Lenses, open Lens Scheduler, make a Lens Group, add the shirt Lens to it, and copy the group's Lens Group ID.
    6. Open App Keys, paste the token and the Lens Group ID, and press Save Snap keys. Then try AR Try-On in the app.
    For your developer

    Open lens-studio/FitFusionShirts in Lens Studio → Publish Lens → the shop's Organization → a new Lens Folder. "Save as Draft" only goes through automatic checks. App Keys has the detailed steps and the optional Lens ID. Fitted garments that bend with the body are added in Lens Studio; caps, glasses and bags are added on the 3D Models page.

    Open App Keys

    GCash payments (PayMongo)

    Customers pay with GCash through PayMongo, and the money goes to the shop's PayMongo account, then to the shop's bank.

    No monthly fee; PayMongo takes a small cut of each paymentYou, then your developera few days for verification
    1. Sign up at paymongo.com in the shop's name.
    2. Finish PayMongo's business verification (they ask for business documents). Until then only test payments work.
    3. Your developer puts the shop's payment key into the app and makes a new app file. Give it to them in person or by phone, never by chat or email.
    4. Place one real order with GCash to check that the money arrives.
    The PayMongo "secret key" can move money. Never paste it on this site, in a chat, or in an email.

    Part 3 · Every day

    what running the shop looks like

    A normal day

    1. Check the Dashboard: it lists what needs you today.
    2. Open new orders in Orders, check each item's picture and design (click the order to open it), and move the status along as you print.
    3. When it's ready to go, assign a driver on the order. It shows the address, the distance and directions.
    4. When stock arrives, add it in Products & Stock → In storage.
    5. Once a month, a Super Admin downloads a backup from Advanced → Export.
